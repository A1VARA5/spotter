## Major

### M1. A late CAPTURE.PENDING (or COMPLETED) after CAPTURE.DECLINED marks a declined payment as paid
- **Where:** `src/paypal/webhook.ts:21-26` (rules), `src/paypal/webhook.ts:103-109` (act)
- **Severity:** major (money path: Spotter says money moved when PayPal declined it)
- **Scenario:** The spotter approves. The capture comes back PENDING (an eCheck or a risk review), so the payment is `captured` with capture C9. PayPal then declines C9. `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.DECLINED` arrives and moves the payment `captured -> failed`, which is right. But PayPal doesn't guarantee delivery order, and the earlier `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.PENDING` delivery failed and is retried after the decline. Its rule lists `failed` in `from` (that's there for "failed after a timeout"), so it moves the payment back to `captured`. The dashboard, Watchtower and the allowance now count a payment PayPal declined as paid. COMPLETED has the same `from` list. The bug was latent with `DENIED`. The DECLINED fix makes it reachable, because DECLINED is the name Orders v2 actually sends.
- **Proof:** `webhook.review.test.ts` > "a late PAYMENT.CAPTURE.PENDING for a capture PayPal already DECLINED doesn't turn the payment back into 'paid'"
- **Fix:** A decline is final for that capture id. In `act()`, for CAPTURE.PENDING and CAPTURE.COMPLETED, refuse to settle (record it as a `paypal_mismatch`) when `paypal_events` already holds a `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.DECLINED` or `DENIED` for the same `resource_id`. Or store the declined capture id on the payment and check it in `settleFromPayPal`'s WHERE clause.

### M2. A price drop is marked "told" before the spotter is told; a failed or cut-off notice is never retried
- **Where:** `src/core/watch.ts:347-357` (checkWatch), `src/server/watch-notify.ts:145-146`
- **Severity:** major (silent failure of the feature's main promise)
- **Scenario:** `checkWatch` first sets `status = 'dropped', notified_at = now()`, then calls `notify`. Here's how the notice gets lost:
  - (a) All channels fail (Telegram or Discord 5xx, email timeout). The error is logged and swallowed.
  - (b) The function is killed between the two steps. The cron route has `maxDuration = 60` but runs `reconcileStuck` (up to 30 PayPal lookups with 15 s timeouts) and then up to 40 s of watches. The webhook route has `maxDuration = 30` but can run 5 checks in a row, each up to 8+8 s of Channel3 calls, a 15 s notify and an 8 s stop. See m3.

  Either way the row says `dropped` and `notified_at` is set. The daily job only polls `status = 'watching'`, so nothing ever tries again. The spotter only finds out if they happen to open the dashboard.
- **Proof:** `watch.review.test.ts` > "a drop whose notice failed to send is tried again, not left marked as told"
- **Fix:** Set `status = 'dropped'` in the conditional update, but write `notified_at` only after `notify` succeeds. Have `runWatches` retry `status = 'dropped' and notified_at is null` (with a retry cap or age limit).

## Minor

### m1. A Channel3 subscription can be stopped while another family's new watch on the same product is being saved
- **Where:** `src/core/watch.ts:195-209` (release), `src/core/watch.ts:237-251` (startWatch: Channel3 start, then history, then insert)
- **Scenario:** Family B's `startWatch` has called `c3.start(P)` and is waiting on `c3.history(P)`; its row isn't inserted yet. Meanwhile family A stops its watch on P (or A's watch drops). `release` finds no other `watching` row and calls `c3.stop(P)`. Then B's row is inserted with `tracking = true`. Channel3 isn't tracking P any more and nothing will start it again. B gets no webhooks and Channel3 keeps no price history for P. Drops are still found by the daily poll, up to about 24 h late.
- **Proof:** `watch.review.test.ts` > "a family that stops its watch while another family is starting one on the same product doesn't switch Channel3 tracking off under it"
- **Fix:** Insert the row first, with `tracking = false` and a pending state, before calling `c3.start`, then mark it `tracking = true`. `release` then sees the pending row. Or take a per-product advisory lock (`pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext(product_id))`) around start+insert and release.

### m2. A Channel3 subscription is left running forever if `/api/watches` is cut off between `c3.start` and the insert
- **Where:** `src/core/watch.ts:227-281`, `src/app/api/watches/route.ts:7` (`maxDuration = 30`)
- **Scenario:** search (up to 8 s), start (up to 8 s) and history (up to 8 s) can take about 24 s before the insert. With a slow Channel3, Vercel kills the function at 30 s, after `start` succeeded and before the row exists. The `.catch` only covers a thrown insert. The daily job only knows about rows, so it never stops this subscription: 1 credit per day, forever.
- **Proof:** unproven (a platform kill can't be simulated in vitest)
- **Fix:** The same row-first ordering as m1 fixes this too. As a safety net, the daily job can call Channel3's list-subscriptions (`docs/channel3/api-reference_price-tracking_list-subscriptions.md`) and stop any product with no `watching` row.

### m3. The daily job's time budget isn't a deadline, and doesn't count the reconcile before it
- **Where:** `src/core/watch.ts:393-432` (`budgetMs = 40_000`, checked only before each watch), `src/app/api/cron/reconcile/route.ts:7` (`maxDuration = 60`), `src/app/api/channel3/webhook/route.ts:7` (`maxDuration = 30`) with `HINT_BATCH = 5` (`watch.ts:22`)
- **Scenario:** A watch that starts at 39.9 s can run another ~39 s: product 8 s, history 8 s, notify up to 15 s, stop 8 s. The reconcile before it can take its own 30 x 15 s. Vercel kills the route, the response is a 504 with no summary, and a kill mid-drop hits M2.
- **Proof:** `watch.review.test.ts` > "stops polling within its time budget, so the cron route (maxDuration 60, reconcile runs first) isn't killed mid check" (45 s spent against a 40 s budget)
- **Fix:** Pass a deadline (`until`) instead of a budget. Subtract the reconcile's elapsed time in `handleReconcile`. Skip starting a check when less than about 25 s is left. Give the webhook route the same deadline, or check at most one watch per delivery.

### m4. Peek's daily caps don't count starts whose browser really was created
- **Where:** `src/server/peek.ts:185-186` (`close_reason is distinct from 'failed'`), `src/server/peek.ts:228-235`
- **Scenario:** When `browsers.create` takes longer than 12 s (KERNEL is slow, or the start page is a slow server the payer controls, since the URL comes from the payment's own message), the start is marked `failed` and doesn't count. The browser still gets created and is deleted when it arrives. A demo family (free to make) can keep pressing "Look again" and create unlimited billed KERNEL browsers. The global cap of 30 doesn't help, because it excludes `failed` too.
- **Proof:** `peek.review.test.ts` > "counts a start whose cloud browser was really created towards the daily cap, even when it arrived too late" (6 browsers against a cap of 3)
- **Fix:** Count `failed` rows too, or mark a timed-out start `late` (counted) and keep `failed` only for a create that KERNEL refused.

### m5. Price watch caps: one family (or one agent key) can use up every family's daily allowance of new watches
- **Where:** `src/core/watch.ts:190-192` (`CAP_WHERE`)
- **Scenario:** The per-family cap only counts `watching` rows. The global per-day cap (40) counts every row created today. Start, stop, start, stop keeps a family under its 5 while it uses up the 40 for everyone. That includes the `watch_price` MCP tool with free `item` and `price_pence`. Judges using fresh demo families then see "Spotter is watching as many prices as it can today". Each start also spends a Channel3 credit.
- **Proof:** `watch.review.test.ts` > "one family can't use up the whole day's new watches for every other family by starting and stopping"
- **Fix:** Add a per-family per-day cap on new watches (for example 10, counting every status, not examples).

### m6. A drop can be announced after the watch has expired
- **Where:** `src/core/watch.ts:325` (checkWatch checks status but not `expires_at`), `src/core/watch.ts:371-377` (hintFromWebhook)
- **Scenario:** Expiry is only applied by the daily job at 04:17 UTC. For up to 24 h after `expires_at`, a webhook hint can still mark a drop and notify. The Channel3 subscription also keeps costing credits until the job runs.
- **Proof:** `watch.review.test.ts` > "doesn't announce a drop on a watch that has already run past its 14 days"
- **Fix:** Add `and expires_at > now()` to `checkWatch`'s guard and to the drop update. Expire and release in `hintFromWebhook` when it sees an expired row.

### m7. The simple view shows nothing for a person whose saved name has doubled spaces
- **Where:** `src/watchtower/gran.ts:12, 21` (`clean()` collapses whitespace), `gran.ts:67` (`r.who === o.who`), `gran.ts:259` (page filter value)
- **Scenario:** `granLabels` normalises the name ("Margaret  Smith" becomes "Margaret Smith"; `gran.test.ts` even tests padded names). Payment rows carry the raw `spenders.name`. Setup trims the ends (zod `.trim()`) but not doubled spaces inside a name. So the board's `payments.who equals` filter and the `my_month` tool both match nothing. Gran is told nothing was stopped: a silent "nothing found".
- **Proof:** `gran.review.test.ts` > "finds the person's payments when their saved name has extra spaces (the label trims it, the rows don't)"
- **Fix:** Keep the raw name for filtering (`who`) and use the cleaned name for display only. Or normalise names on save.

### m8. The simple view reads dollar payments out as pounds
- **Where:** `src/watchtower/gran.ts:105, 111-113`; `src/watchtower/data.ts:127` (`pounds()` drops the currency); `data.ts:79-83` (`toWatchRows`); `widgetLogic.ts` `watchSummary` (adds savings across currencies)
- **Scenario:** A business, or a family of agents only, gets the everyone view. An agent with a dollar allowance has a $119 payment stopped, and the `my_month` tool tells the person "£119.00". The pattern was already there in Watchtower (every amount is "in pounds"); Gran and price watch inherit it.
- **Proof:** `gran.review.test.ts` > "doesn't read a dollar payment out as pounds"
- **Fix:** Carry `currency` on `PaymentRow` and `WatchRow` and format with it. Don't add up across currencies (show one total per currency).

### m9. Channel3's price history line is thrown away when one answer has fewer than two points
- **Where:** `src/core/watch.ts:157-173` (mergeHistory)
- **Scenario:** The previous history came from Channel3 (30 points). Today Channel3 answers with one point in GBP (or a short window). `theirs.length < 2`, and the previous line isn't `checks`, so it starts again from one point. The sparkline collapses to "fills in as Spotter checks each day".
- **Proof:** `watch.review.test.ts` > "keeps Channel3's history line when one answer has fewer than two points in the family's currency"
- **Fix:** When `prev.source === "channel3"` and the fresh answer is short, keep `prev` and append the new point.

### m10. Closing the peek panel in a second tab ends the look running in the first tab
- **Where:** `src/ui/Peek.tsx:37-43` (`close()` always calls `stop()`), `Peek.tsx:108`
- **Scenario:** Tab 1 is watching the page. In tab 2 the same card's "Look at it safely" gets 409 ("already looking"). The code rightly doesn't call `stop()` for a 409. But pressing Close or Escape in tab 2 then POSTs `/peek/close`, and `closePeek` closes the household's open peek on that payment: tab 1's look ends and its browser is deleted.
- **Proof:** unproven (the test setup has no jsdom; a code read only)
- **Fix:** Track whether this panel opened a look (a ref set on a 200 from start) and only send `/close` when it did, or when the start's answer is still unknown.

### m11. Smaller notes (no test)
- `src/ui/Peek.tsx:88`: "Look again" calls `showModal()` on a dialog that's already open. Current browsers return quietly; older Safari threw `InvalidStateError`, which would leave the panel stuck on "Opening". Guard it with `if (!dialog.current?.open)`.
- `src/server/peek.ts:276-279`: the frame slot update doesn't check `closed_at is null`. It's harmless today because `livePeek` checked first, but a close in between still costs one screenshot.
- `src/server/peek.ts:285-290`: with no `content-length` header, the whole body is buffered before the 3 MB check. Read it as a stream with a byte limit.
- `src/watchtower/gran.ts:62`: "this month" is a rolling 30 days, not the calendar month the widgets say.
- `src/app/watchtower/Studio.tsx` `switchTo`: switching to the simple view while the spotter's own assistant is still running unmounts Studio mid run. Only Gran's busy state blocks the switch, not the spotter harness's.
- `src/market/webhook.ts:36`: with `CHANNEL3_WEBHOOK_SECRET` unset, unsigned deliveries are accepted. They're only a hint, rate limited by the 10 minute quiet window, but make sure the secret is set in production.

## Checked and fine

- PayPal DECLINED rule: the capture id guard (`webhook.ts:103`) protects a different successful capture. The only "captured without a capture id" path is `reconcileStuck`, where a DECLINED really does mean nothing was paid.
- Peek: ownership (`getPayment` with household), the page must come from the payment's own message, private address check, one open peek per household (partial unique index), and sealed session ids. Browsers are deleted on close, failed start, late arrival, sweep and unmount, with sendBeacon on pagehide. Close racing start is handled: the update after close returns 409 and the browser is deleted in `finally`.
- Price watch: drop decided by one conditional update (no double notify), webhook price never trusted, an outage isn't treated as "no product", a failed stop is retried by the daily job, and deleted demo families get their subscriptions stopped.
- Gran agent: one read only tool, no ids in its output, and payee names passed as data.
